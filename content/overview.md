# Info

This page contains a small breakdown about everything you need to know about this world and the main story.

## World

- This world is a general fantasy world with a medieval setting.
- The world was created by [[“The God”]]

## Demigods

- Demigods appeared about a year after [[“The Gods”]] disappearance; the 4 Demigods claim to be the children of [[“The God”]]
- Each demigod resembles a core concept of the universe. The Demigods are:
    - [[Venus (Vita), Demigod of Life]]
    - [[Mortem (Misho), Demigod of Death]]
    - [[Spatium (Sienna), Demigod of Space]]
    - [[Tempus (Teodus), Demigod of Time]]
- Each Demigod has a “[[church]]” dedicated to them. It’s unknown if Demigods can die.

## Timeline

- **300 years ago:** [[“The God”]] dissappeared.

- **299 years ago:** The [[Demigods]] appeared.

- **200 years ago:** [[The original kingdom]] is created.

- **50 years ago:** [[Mortem (Misho), Demigod of Death]] runs away from after an unknown incident.

- **2 years ago:**  [[Spatium (Sienna), Demigod of Space]] gets a vision about Mortem’s location.

- **1 year ago:** The three other Demigods go with a group of citizens on an expedition to search for [[Mortem (Misho), Demigod of Death]].

- **0 years ago:** (start of server) The group arrives at the location [[Spatium (Sienna), Demigod of Space]] saw in her vision.
